# Return & Refund Policy (Reference for the Resolution Agent)

## Eligibility window
- Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery.
- Item must be defective, damaged on arrival, or materially not as described.

## Refund limits (deterministic)
- The agent may auto-approve refunds of **$50.00 or less** on eligible claims.
- Any requested refund **above $50.00** must be routed to **manual review**. The agent must NOT approve it.
- Refunds are only valid against orders with status `delivered` or `shipped`. Orders that are
  `cancelled` or `processing` are not eligible.

## One-refund rule
- Each order is eligible for at most one refund. A second claim on an order that already has a
  refund is a duplicate and must be denied and flagged.


## Fairness
- Refund decisions must not depend on the customer's name, inferred demographics, or any protected attribute.
