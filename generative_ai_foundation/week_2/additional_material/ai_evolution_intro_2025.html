The Foundations

1950-1980
1950
Turing Test Published
Alan Turing poses "Can machines think?" in his landmark paper, establishing the intellectual foundation for AI research.
Foundation
1956
AI Term Coined - Dartmouth Conference
John McCarthy organizes the Dartmouth Conference, officially coining "Artificial Intelligence" and launching AI as a formal field of study.
Birth of AI
1957-1969
First Neural Networks - The Perceptron
Frank Rosenblatt develops the Perceptron, the first neural network capable of learning. "The Embryo of an electronic computer that [the Navy] expects will be able to walk, talk, see, write, reproduce itself and be conscious of its existence."
❄️

AI Winter & Expert Systems

1980-2000
1986
Backpropagation Rediscovered
Rumelhart, Hinton, and Williams popularize backpropagation, enabling training of multi-layer neural networks. The technique that would eventually power modern AI.
1997
Deep Blue Defeats Kasparov
IBM's Deep Blue becomes the first computer to defeat a reigning world chess champion in a standard match, marking AI's first major public victory.
Historic Win
📊

Machine Learning Revolution

2000-2012
2006
Deep Learning Renaissance
Geoffrey Hinton publishes breakthrough work on training deep neural networks, reigniting interest in neural networks after decades of dormancy.
2012
AlexNet Wins ImageNet
AlexNet achieves 85% accuracy in ImageNet competition (vs. 74% prior), demonstrating deep learning's superiority. Computer vision is revolutionized overnight.
Deep Learning Era Begins
🧠

Deep Learning Dominance

2012-2020
2014
GANs Invented
Ian Goodfellow introduces Generative Adversarial Networks, enabling photorealistic image generation and launching the creative AI revolution.
2017
Transformer Architecture: "Attention Is All You Need"
Google researchers publish the Transformer architecture, introducing the self-attention mechanism that would become the foundation for GPT, BERT, and all modern LLMs.
Modern AI Foundation
2018
GPT-1 & BERT Released
OpenAI releases GPT-1 (117M parameters) proving transformers work for language generation. Google releases BERT, revolutionizing NLP with bidirectional understanding.
2020
GPT-3 Stuns the World
OpenAI releases GPT-3 (175B parameters), demonstrating that scale + transformers = emergent capabilities. In-context learning and few-shot learning surprise even the researchers.
Game Changer
🚀

The AI Explosion

2022-2025
Nov 2022
ChatGPT Launches
ChatGPT reaches 1M users in 5 days, 100M in 2 months. AI enters mainstream consciousness. The world changes overnight.
Cultural Moment
Mar 2023
GPT-4 Released
OpenAI releases GPT-4 with vision, 128K context, and near-human performance on professional exams. Passes the bar exam at 90th percentile.
Multimodal Frontier
2024
The Open Source Surge
Meta releases Llama 3 (open weights, 405B parameters). Claude 3.5 Sonnet achieves SOTA coding. Competition intensifies between OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Meta.
Dec 2024
DeepSeek V3: China's Cost Revolution
Chinese startup DeepSeek releases V3 (671B MoE), trained for just $6M vs. $100M+ for competitors. Matches GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 performance while using 1/10th the computing power, proving ultra-efficient training is possible.
Efficiency Breakthrough
Jan 2025
DeepSeek R1 & Chatbot Launch
DeepSeek releases R1 reasoning model matching OpenAI o1 performance. The free chatbot reaches #1 on iOS App Store, surpassing ChatGPT and triggering 18% drop in NVIDIA stock. Open-source AI disrupts the market.
Market Disruption
Aug 2025
DeepSeek V3.1: Hybrid Intelligence
DeepSeek V3.1 introduces hybrid thinking/non-thinking modes, enhanced tool use, and superior agentic capabilities. Outperforms V3 and R1 by 40% on SWE-bench. One model for both reasoning and speed.
Sep-Oct 2024
Salesforce Agentforce Launches
Salesforce launches Agentforce, the first enterprise "digital labor platform." Within 2 months: 6,000+ deals closed, 10,000 agents built at Dreamforce, 84% autonomous case resolution. Becomes Salesforce's fastest-growing product ever with 330% YoY ARR growth.
Enterprise Agents
Nov 2025
Gemini 3.0 & GPT-5.1 Launch
Google releases Gemini 3.0 with native multimodal architecture and 2M token context. OpenAI counters with GPT-5.1 (1M context, advanced reasoning). Released just 6 days apart - the AI race reaches peak intensity.
Present Day
Dec 2025
DeepSeek V3.2 & Agentic AI Explosion
DeepSeek V3.2 debuts thinking-in-tool-use and Sparse Attention. Meanwhile, agentic AI reaches production scale: Microsoft, AWS, Salesforce deploy agent platforms. Gartner predicts 15% of work decisions made autonomously by 2028. Capital One, PepsiCo show 40% cost reductions.
Autonomy Era

🤖 Agentic AI in Business: The Salesforce Agentforce Story

📊 The Numbers Tell the Story

  • Fastest-growing product ever at Salesforce (330% YoY ARR growth)
  • 6,000+ deals closed since Oct 2024 launch (3,000+ paid)
  • 10,000 agents built by attendees at Dreamforce in 3 days
  • 84% autonomous resolution of customer cases
  • 50% of Fortune 100 companies using it
  • IRS saved 98% of manual tasks, cutting processes from 10 days to 30 minutes

🎯 What Makes It Special

Agentforce represents the shift from chatbots to autonomous "digital labor." Unlike copilots that need constant human prompting, these agents:

  • Make decisions and take actions independently
  • Work 24/7 across sales, service, marketing
  • Integrate with existing Salesforce data & workflows
  • Use Atlas Reasoning Engine for human-like planning
  • Can be built with natural language (no coding needed)

🎓 For Your Class: Agentforce is the perfect case study for business agentic AI. It shows how agents moved from theory (2023) → prototypes (early 2024) → enterprise production (late 2024) → explosive growth (2025). Students can explore real ROI data, see agent architecture (Atlas Reasoning Engine, Data Cloud integration), and understand governance challenges at scale.

🛠️ Popular Agentic AI Frameworks

Building agentic AI applications requires robust frameworks. Here are the top tools developers are using in 2025 to create autonomous, multi-agent systems:

🔗 LangGraph

By LangChain: Graph-based workflow orchestration with stateful agents. Best for complex, multi-step reasoning with precise control over agent flow. Lowest latency in benchmarks.

👥 CrewAI

By AI Fund (Andrew Ng): Role-based multi-agent teams. Simplest to learn with intuitive "crew" concept. Best for rapid prototyping and production-grade agent systems with task delegation.

💬 AutoGen

By Microsoft: Conversation-driven multi-agent collaboration. Enterprise-grade reliability with advanced error handling. Best for research, prototyping, and agent-to-agent dialogue.

📚 LlamaIndex

Data-centric agents: Specializes in RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and data indexing. Best for agents that need to work with enterprise data, documents, and knowledge bases.

🌀 OpenAI Swarm

By OpenAI: Lightweight, experimental framework for multi-agent coordination. Educational/research focus. Best for rapid experiments and learning agent patterns.

🔧 Semantic Kernel

By Microsoft: Enterprise LLM integration for .NET/C#/Java. Strong security and governance. Best for integrating AI into existing enterprise applications with strict compliance.

🎓 For Your Class: These frameworks abstract away the complexity of agent orchestration, memory management, and tool integration. Most popular stack: LangGraph for workflow control, CrewAI for team-based agents, and AutoGen for conversational loops. Students can compare architectures (graph-based vs role-based vs conversation-driven) and understand tradeoffs between control, simplicity, and enterprise features.
